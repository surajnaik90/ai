# Artificial Intelligence

1. What is Intelligence?   
   **Ability to learn, understand and apply knowledge to solve problems.**   

2. What is Artifical intelligence?   
   **Simulation of human intelligence by computers, machines to learn, apply knowledge to solve problems.**   

3. What is Machine Learning (ML)?   
   **It is a subset of AI focused on building systems that identify patterns from data    
     to make decisions, predictions.**   

4. What is Deep Learning (DL)?   
   **It is a subset of Machine Learning (ML) based on neural networks (modeled around the working of a human brain)   
     with a focus to analyze complex datasets like images, audio etc. mimicking human brain functionality**
